// how it works
01
Upload your model

Drop your TorchScript (.pt) or ONNX (.onnx) model. AO8 validates it loads correctly before running anything.

02
Upload your dataset

Upload a CSV with numeric features. The last column is treated as the label. AO8 normalises values automatically.

03
Choose your attacks

Select FGSM, PGD, or C&W. Set the perturbation budget (epsilon). AO8 queues the job and runs it in the background.

04
Read the report

See exactly which samples flipped, which features were exploited, your risk score, and download the safe values.

// understand the attacks
FAST GRADIENT SIGN METHOD
FGSM

The simplest attack. Takes one step in the direction that maximises the model's loss. Very fast, but not the strongest. Good for a quick sanity check.

Learn more →
PROJECTED GRADIENT DESCENT
PGD

Runs FGSM iteratively, projecting back into an epsilon-ball after each step. The standard benchmark attack. If your model survives PGD, it's meaningfully robust.
